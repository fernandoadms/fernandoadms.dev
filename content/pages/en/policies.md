This policy explains what data **fernandoadms.dev** processes when you visit the site, why, and how you control it. It follows the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and, for visitors in the EEA and the UK, the GDPR

## Who is the controller

The data controller is Fernando Alves. For anything privacy related, contact [fernando.alves0189@gmail.com](mailto:fernando.alves0189@gmail.com)

## What the site collects

The site has no user accounts, comments or forms. The data sources are:

- **Hosting:** the server logs technical data for each request, such as IP address, browser and requested page, for security and to keep the site running
- **Analytics (Google Analytics 4):** when enabled, it measures which pages are read and **only loads after you consent**
- **Ads:** the site shows no ads

## Local storage

The site uses your browser's localStorage to remember your theme and consent choices. This data stays on your device

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to [fernando.alves0189@gmail.com](mailto:fernando.alves0189@gmail.com). Click **Cookie preferences** in the footer to change or withdraw your consent at any time
